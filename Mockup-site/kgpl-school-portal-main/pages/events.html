Annual Day

A whole-school celebration of the year's achievements — cultural performances, prize distribution, and felicitations.

Teachers' Day

Students honor their teachers, with senior students taking on classroom duties for the day.

Children's Day

Games, treats, and special activities organized by teachers for every student.

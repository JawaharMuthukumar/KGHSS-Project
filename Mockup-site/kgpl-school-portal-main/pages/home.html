About Our School

A legacy of government education in Kangayampalayam

Government Higher Secondary School, Kangayampalayam has served the local community since 1926, providing free, quality education to students of classes 6 through 12 under the Tamil Nadu State Board curriculum, with dedicated teachers and a nurturing campus. This year marks our 100th year.

Headmaster's Profile

CD

C.Martin Devesagayam

Headmaster, GHSS Kangayampalayam

C.Martin Devesagayam has served Government Higher Secondary School, Kangayampalayam for over two decades, and has led the school as Headmaster since 2021. Under his guidance, the school has strengthened its academic results, expanded student welfare programmes, and modernised campus safety with full CCTV coverage.

He is a strong believer in discipline, community partnership, and equal access to quality education for every child in Kangayampalayam, regardless of background.

A Message to Our School Community

"Our mission is to provide every child in Kangayampalayam access to disciplined, quality education regardless of background. A good teacher builds good students, and good students grow into tomorrow's leaders — that belief has guided this school for a century. As we celebrate our centenary in 2026–2027, I want every parent to know that their child's safety, character, and academic growth remain our first priority. We take pride in our students' achievements and our teachers' dedication, and we welcome every family to be part of this journey."

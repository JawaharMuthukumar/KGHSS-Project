A Safe, Modern Campus

Facilities & Safety

Student Safety

Monitored, Connected Classrooms

CCTV Surveillance — Every Classroom

All classrooms are under continuous CCTV monitoring, giving the school office and parents confidence that every child is safe throughout the school day.

Classroom Intercom & Mic System

Every classroom is fitted with its own microphone, letting the Headmaster speak directly to students in any class and hear from them in real time — for announcements, discipline, or a quick word of encouragement.

Qualified Teaching Staff

Every subject is taught by a trained, qualified teacher, backed by regular in-service training from the School Education Department.

Tamil & English Medium — Separate Classes

Tamil medium and English medium students study in their own dedicated classrooms, each taught fully in their medium of instruction.

Labs & Learning Spaces

Hands-On Science & Technology

Computer Lab
Digital literacy and computer science practicals for every class.
Biology Lab
Fully equipped for Group II Biology practicals and NEET preparation.
Chemistry Lab
Dedicated space for Chemistry experiments across Groups I & II.
Beyond the Classroom

Extra Curriculars

Sports

Athletics and team sports on our dedicated sports ground, building fitness and team spirit.

Performing Arts

Music, dance and drama practice for Annual Day and cultural celebrations through the year.

Fine Arts

Drawing, craft and the annual Science & Subject Exhibition projects students design themselves.

Clubs & Camps

NSS, NCC and the Eco Club — community service, discipline and our green campus, all led by students.

Our Green Campus

A Living, Labeled Medicinal Garden

As an Eco Club green-zone school, we've planted a variety of trees and medicinal plants across the campus — each one labeled so students can learn its name and use just by walking past it.

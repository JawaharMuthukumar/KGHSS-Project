Learning Democracy

Student Council & School Committees

Student Leadership Elections

Students Vote For Their Own Leaders

Every year, we hold a real school election so students learn about democracy first-hand — nominations, campaigning, voting and results, just like a real election.

SPL
School Pupil Leader

The senior-most elected student leader, representing the whole student body.

ASPL
Assistant School Pupil Leader

Supports the SPL and steps in across school activities and events.

JSPL
Junior School Pupil Leader

Represents the junior classes, giving younger students an early taste of leadership.

School Committees & Organizations

Parents, teachers, alumni and the community all play a part in running this school through these bodies.

MMS
Munnal Manavar Sangam — our alumni association, connecting former students back to the school.
PTA
Parent-Teacher Association — parents and teachers working together for every child's progress.
SMC
School Management Committee — oversees school administration and development priorities.
SWA
Students' Welfare Association — supports student wellbeing initiatives across the school.

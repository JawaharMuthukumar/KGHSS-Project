Our Journey

A Century of Milestones

1926

School Founded

Government Higher Secondary School, Kangayampalayam opens its doors as a small village primary school, serving the local farming community.

1950s

Upgraded to Middle School

Rising enrollment leads to an upgrade from primary to middle school status, adding classes through grade 8.

1968

Upgraded to Full High School

The school is upgraded to a full High School, extending education through grade 10 for the first time.

1990s

Higher Secondary Status

Classes 11 and 12 are introduced as the school becomes a Higher Secondary institution, with the Science stream added soon after.

2000s–2010s

Labs Modernized

Science and computer laboratories are modernized, bringing digital literacy and hands-on experimentation to every student.

2026–27

Centenary Year

A full year of celebrations marking 100 years of government education in Kangayampalayam — alumni gatherings, cultural events, and community outreach.

A Guiding Belief

Good teachers build good students

For a hundred years, one idea has guided this school: a good teacher builds good students, and good students grow into tomorrow's leaders. That belief shaped every classroom, every playground, and every generation of alumni who passed through these gates — in Sports, NSS, NCC, and the Eco Club alike.

Headmaster's Message

"As we mark our centenary, we celebrate not just a hundred years of buildings and classrooms, but a hundred years of teachers who believed in every child who walked through our gates. May the next century be just as proud."
